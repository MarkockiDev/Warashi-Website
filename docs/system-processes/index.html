The System helper

Install the optional System helper so Warashi can include WindowServer and other system processes, set charge limits below 80%, and keep your Mac awake with the lid closed.

Some things need a little more access than an ordinary app has. The optional System helper provides exactly three of them, and nothing else:

  • System processes: WindowServer, kernel_task, mds and friends, in Apps, the busiest apps and History.
  • Charge limits below 80% (Battery & charge limit).
  • Staying awake with the lid closed (Keep Awake).
  1. Open Settings › System

    Press ⌘, and choose System. Warashi also offers Turn On the first time you choose something that needs it.

  2. Turn on Show system processes

    Warashi installs the small helper. macOS then asks for your approval.

  3. Approve it in System Settings

    Click Open Login Items Settings in Warashi, or open System Settings › General › Login Items. Under Allow in the Background, turn on Warashi. macOS may ask for your password.

What the helper can and can't do

  • It reads process counters: CPU time, memory, disk and energy.
  • Only when you ask, it sets one charge level between 20% and 75%, which it checks itself, or keeps your Mac awake with the lid closed on a three-minute lease that it ends by itself if Warashi stops renewing it.
  • It takes no other commands, no paths and no arguments, and answers only Warashi (both sides check each other's signature).
  • Turn the switch off to remove it.

If something's not right

  • Still no system processes: check that Warashi is on under System Settings › General › Login Items › Allow in the Background, then turn the switch off and on again.
