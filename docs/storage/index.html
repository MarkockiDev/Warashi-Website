Storage is a tab in Health. It shows what's using your startup disk. It opens with your last results and updates them if they're more than six hours old. It never deletes anything.

  1. Open Storage

    Choose Health, then Storage, press the Disk tile on Now or in the menu bar window, or Open on Checkup's storage card.

  2. Switch on Full Disk Access

    If you didn't do this in the welcome tour, a card offers it. Press Open System Settings and turn on Warashi in the list, once. That lets Storage include Desktop, Documents, Downloads, photos and app data. Warashi reads sizes only, never your files, and nothing leaves your Mac. You can skip this: Storage measures the rest and macOS never interrupts.

  3. Explore the ring

    The inner ring is your categories, plus System and Free. The outer ring is the biggest folders in each. Point at any part to see its name and size; the rest dim and its row lights up in the list.

  4. See what's inside

    Choose a category to see its largest items. The magnifying glass shows one in Finder, where you can decide what to do with it.

  5. Scan whenever you like

    Press Scan Again at any time. You can leave the page while it scans.

What the categories mean

  • Developer: Xcode data and Simulators.
  • App data: everything in your Library folder, apart from Developer.
  • Trash: what's waiting to be emptied.
  • Other home folders: anything else directly in your home folder, such as a Code folder.
  • System and other: what's left: macOS itself, snapshots, and places Warashi can't read.
Good to know: Folder sizes can overlap a little, because files that share space on disk are counted in each place.

If something's not right

  • Some places need Full Disk Access: press that line in the list, then Open System Settings. If Warashi isn't in the list, drag its icon in. If it still isn't counted, press Reopen Warashi.
