Warashi's notch shows only what's going on right now. When nothing is, the notch is just the notch. While an agent works, a break is coming up, Keep Awake is on, the battery charges to your limit, a speed test runs or Soft Focus is on, the most important of them sits left of the notch and the next right of it, each with its symbol and colour from Tools. A small number on the right one counts any more.

On displays without a notch, such as an external monitor, a small black island does the same where a notch would be. When nothing's going on, it steps away.

When something needs you

With the notch or the island on, Warashi tells you things from there instead of a notification in the corner of your screen. The notch opens by itself with what happened in plain words, its edge lit in the notice's colour, then closes after a few seconds. The notice then waits beside the notch as its symbol, ahead of everything else, until you deal with it, dismiss it, or it's no longer true.

An alert, such as Your Mac is running hot or Chrome is using a lot of CPUTell Me More opens the page that explains it; an app using a lot of CPU or memory can be quit right there, and a stuck compiler stopped

Each notice has a small × to dismiss it. Without a notch or island, alerts arrive as notifications and the memory and Trash cards drop from Warashi's menu bar item, as before.

Open it

  1. Rest the pointer on the notch

    Hover the notch (or where the island sits) for a moment and it opens downward: each moment as a row with its controls, and one line on your Mac underneath (CPU, memory, battery). With nothing going on, it offers Keep Awake, a break and Charge to Full.

  2. Move away to close it

    Move the pointer away and it closes. Click it, or Open Warashi in its corner, to open the window.

Choose how it looks

In Settings › Customize › Always Showing: Show what's going on beside the notch and Island on displays without a notch switch each one off completely. Readings when nothing's going on puts two numbers of your choice beside a calm notch, CPU and memory unless you pick others. See Customize.

With another notch app

Other apps can use the notch too, and each one watches the pointer on its own, so pointing at the notch can open both. Warashi's notch stays on top and answers the pointer. Settings › Customize › Always Showing names any other app using the notch; to see only Warashi, turn off the notch in that app. Warashi never changes the other app.

Good to know: Not sure what a notice means? Every one has Tell Me More, which opens the page in Warashi that explains it and shows the rest of what you can do.

If something's not right

  • The island covers something on your external display: turn off Island on displays without a notch in Settings › Customize › Always Showing.
  • Keep Awake came on by itself: point at the notch to see why. While an agent works means the rule in Settings › Coding Agents is on.
