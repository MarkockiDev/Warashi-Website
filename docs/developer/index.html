Choose Developer in the sidebar or press ⌘7. It reads your ports and agents only while it's on screen.

Dev servers

Servers are grouped by the folder they were started in, with their ports, CPU and memory. Open opens one in your browser, Copy copies its address, and Quit… asks it to stop after you confirm. △ On your network means other devices on your network may be able to reach it.

AI agents

Each running Claude Code, Codex, Cursor Agent, Gemini CLI, Aider, OpenCode, Amp or Goose session, with its project, how long it's been running, CPU time and memory. Tools an agent has started count as part of it.

Other open ports

Everything else with an open port, such as a local AI model. macOS's own services are hidden.

Good to know: CPU time can't include tools that finished before you opened the page. Two servers started from different subfolders of one project appear as two projects, because Warashi only looks at the folder each was started in.

If something's not right

  • A port is missing: ports held by processes running as administrator can't be read.
  • An agent is missing: agents are recognised from a list of known programs. Tell adam@warashi.app which one you use.

More in Troubleshooting, or email adam@warashi.app.
