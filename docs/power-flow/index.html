Power Flow shows where your Mac's power is coming from and where it's going, right now.

  1. Open it

    On Overview, press the Power Flow button on the Power tile; in the popover, press Power, the reading with a small arrow. Use Overview at the top right to go back.

  2. Read it from left to right

    Power starts at the Charger or Battery, passes through This Mac, and ends at Chip and RAM, USB devices and Everything else, such as the screen and Wi-Fi. A thicker channel carries more.

  3. While it charges

    The charger's channel forks: one branch goes straight to the Battery and the other to This Mac. So the number on This Mac is only what the Mac itself is using.

  4. Focus on one path

    Point at a source or destination to dim the others.

What you'll see

The line at the top saysWhat's happening
Running from the charger. The battery is holdingThe charger runs the Mac, and the battery is neither charging nor draining.
The charger is running this Mac and filling the batteryBoth, with a separate branch to the battery.
Running on batteryThe battery is the only source.
The charger can't keep upThe battery is adding to what the charger delivers.

Below the diagram: the apps that used the most energy in the last 15 minutes, your charger and battery details, and what's connected.

Good to know: These are estimates from separate sensors, averaged over a few seconds, so the two sides may not match exactly. USB devices update about once a minute. Charger and USB power are also recorded: see the Charger chart in History.

If something's not right

  • It says unavailable: Power Flow needs a battery and a charger reading, so it isn't offered on desktop Macs.
  • The light has stopped moving: it holds still with Reduce Motion, in Low Power Mode, or with Settings › General › Lively motion off.
