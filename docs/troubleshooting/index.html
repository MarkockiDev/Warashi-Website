I can't see Warashi in the menu bar

A crowded menu bar can hide items behind the notch. Quit an app or two you don't need in the menu bar, or hold ⌘ and drag the Warashi item further right. You can also open Warashi from Applications at any time: it opens its window.

The island covers something on my external display

Turn it off in Settings › Notch › Island on other displays.

Some numbers say “unavailable”

Warashi never invents a value. If macOS doesn't offer a reading on your Mac (for example temperatures on some models), it says so instead of guessing.

System processes aren't shown

They need the optional helper: see System processes. If you turned it on, check System Settings › General › Login Items › Allow in the Background.

Open at login doesn't stick

macOS may need your approval in System Settings › General › Login Items. Warashi shows a button that takes you there.

Still stuck?

Email adam@warashi.app with your Mac model and macOS version.
