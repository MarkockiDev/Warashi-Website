Find your problem below and follow the steps. Still stuck? Email adam@warashi.app with your Mac model and macOS version.

I can't see Warashi in the menu bar

  1. Check it's running

    Open Warashi from Applications. If it's running, its window opens.

  2. Make room

    On a MacBook with a notch, a crowded menu bar hides items behind the notch. Quit an app or two you don't need in the menu bar, or hold ⌘ and drag the spirit further right.

  3. Use the notch instead

    Turn on Settings › Notch › Glance beside the notch: the numbers there are never hidden.

Some numbers say “unavailable”

Warashi never invents a value. If macOS doesn't offer a reading on your Mac (for example some temperatures or the fan on Macs without one), Warashi says so instead of guessing.

System processes aren't shown

They need the optional helper. Follow System processes, and check that Warashi is allowed under System Settings › General › Login Items › Allow in the Background.

I don't get alert notifications

  1. Check the alert is on

    Settings › Alerts: the switch for that alert must be on.

  2. Allow notifications

    System Settings › Notifications › Warashi › turn on Allow notifications.

Open at login doesn't stick

macOS may need your approval: open System Settings › General › Login Items and allow Warashi.

The island covers something on my external display

Turn off Settings › Notch › Island on displays without a notch.

I want to update right now

Settings › Updates › Check Now. You can also download the newest version from warashi.app and drag it over the old one in Applications; your history stays.

A drive won't eject

macOS refuses while something is using the drive, and Warashi says so. Close whatever is using it and try again.

macOS keeps asking Warashi for access to folders

Update to the newest version in Settings › Updates. Storage now leaves protected folders alone unless you've switched on Full Disk Access once, so there's nothing for macOS to ask about. See Storage.

I changed the popover or notch and want it back

Open Settings › Menu Bar or Notch and press Reset. See Arrange the popover & notch.

A speed test is still running after I left the page

That's on purpose: it finishes in under a minute and the sidebar tells you when it's done. To stop it, go back to Speed Test and press Cancel.

The warashi command isn't found

Open a new Terminal window. If that doesn't help, press Settings › General › Command Line and AI › Install… again.

See the welcome tour again

Settings › General › Show Welcome Again.

Uninstall Warashi

  1. Remove the helper (if you installed it)

    Settings › System › turn off Show system processes.

  2. Quit Warashi

    Click the spirit, then the power button at the bottom right of the popover.

  3. Move it to the Bin

    Drag Warashi from Applications to the Bin.

  4. Remove your history (optional)

    In Finder choose Go › Go to Folder…, enter ~/Library/Application Support/Warashi and move that folder to the Bin.
