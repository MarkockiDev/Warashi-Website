Checkup is the first tab in Health (⌘4). It looks at the last week and answers one question: is your Mac in good shape?

The verdict

At the top: Your Mac is in good shape., or what to look at, in plain words. It's worked out from what Warashi has measured over the last week, and refreshed every few minutes.

What it looks at

CardWhat it showsWhen it asks you to look
BatteryCharge, health, cycles and condition, with the charge limit right beside itHealth under 85%; act under 80%
StorageFree space and what fills your disk, from your last Storage scanUnder 15% free; act under 10 GB
MemoryHow much of the week your Mac spent short of memoryA third of the week or more
TemperatureHow much of the week macOS slowed your Mac to cool it2% of the week or more
Drive healthHow much of your SSD's rated life is used70% or more, or any errors
External drivesThe same for each connected drive that reports its health70% or more, or any errors

Press Memory to see the apps behind it, Temperature for its history, or Open on Storage.

Starts with your Mac

Below, everything that has installed itself to start with your Mac, costliest first, with the energy each used this week. Open Login Items takes you to System Settings to turn any of them off.

Good to know: On Macs with Apple Intelligence, the verdict can be worded on your Mac by Apple's on-device model. It's checked against the facts, and nothing leaves your Mac. Some external drives can't report their health over USB; Checkup says so instead of guessing.
