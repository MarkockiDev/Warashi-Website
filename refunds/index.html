Refund policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

We want you to be happy with Warashi. If you aren't, you can get a full refund within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked.

How

Email adam@warashi.app with your order email, or reply to your Polar receipt. Polar, our merchant of record, returns the payment to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 days. Your licence key is deactivated.

EU consumers

Under EU law you may withdraw from a purchase of digital content within 14 days. Our 14-day refund covers this, even after you've downloaded Warashi and activated your licence.

After 14 days

If something's wrong with Warashi, tell us: we'll try to fix it, and we consider refunds case by case.
