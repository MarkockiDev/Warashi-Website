The cost of not knowing

Every Mac keeps secrets. They get expensive.

Activity Monitor shows this second and forgets it. The things that cost you money and afternoons happen while you aren't looking.

Which app drained the battery?

$249for a new battery

A battery that sits at 100% and warm ages fastest. That's what a MacBook Pro battery costs at Apple once it's worn, out of warranty.

Warashi holds the charge at a level you choose, names the app that drained it, and shows how the battery has aged, month by month.

Why was it slow at 14:02?

4 min× every day

A process runs away, the Mac stutters, you lose the thread. By the time you open Activity Monitor it's over, and tomorrow it happens again.

Warashi writes the slowdown down with its cause, and shows you the app behind it when you click the moment.

Does my next Mac need 32 GB?

$400guessed at checkout

Memory can't be added later. Most people buy too much to be safe, or too little and live with swap for four years.

After 30 days of your real use, the Upgrade Advisor says what your next Mac needs, with the evidence.

How it works

A flight recorder, not a dashboard.

Dashboards show you numbers. A recorder keeps them, so the one time you need the answer, it's already there.

01

It watches, quietly.

One small item in your menu bar. Two numbers beside the notch. A reading every five seconds, costing about 0.2% of one core. You'll forget it's there.

02

It remembers a year.

CPU, memory, power, heat, network, disk and battery, kept on your Mac. Every few seconds for the last day, every minute for a month, every hour for a year.

03

It explains any moment.

Click a spike and Warashi tells you what was going on, in plain words, with the apps that were running and what they usually do. If nothing unusual happened, it says so.

04

It speaks up only when it matters.

A runaway app, memory growing fast, a disk filling up, a battery running hot. Once per problem, with the evidence, and a weekly checkup that says whether your Mac is in good shape.

02 · Power Flow

Zobacz, dokąd trafia każdy wat.

Od ładowarki i baterii, przez Twojego Maca, do układu, urządzeń USB i wszystkiego innego. Podczas ładowania część płynąca do baterii ma własną ścieżkę, więc liczba przy Macu to tylko to, co zużywa sam Mac.

03 · Battery

Stop charging at 80%. Watch the battery age slower.

A battery held at 80% lasts years longer than one that sits at 100%. Warashi sets the limit, charges to full for the one day you need it, and keeps watch.

  • Honest about other appsIf AlDente or another manager also runs, Warashi says so, and the Flight Log notes every time the battery went past your limit and who did it.
  • How it has agedHealth and cycles over the months, from the day you installed it, so you can see whether the limit is working.
  • Power FlowWhat the charger delivers, what the battery takes, what the Mac uses.

04 · Storage

Remove apps properly. Nothing is deleted.

A ring of your whole disk you can step into. Big files, old Downloads with installers marked, local AI models, and a real uninstaller: every app, the ones you haven't opened in months first, with what each has left in your Library.

  • To the Trash, item by itemYou see every item before it moves, and you can put anything back until you empty the Trash.
  • Leftovers of apps long goneFolders from apps you removed years ago, found by their identifier, the safest space on the disk to reclaim.
  • Never your filesNames, sizes and dates only. Full Disk Access is optional, and Warashi never opens a document.

05 · Checkup

Czy mój Mac jest w dobrej formie?

One line, from a week of measurements: battery, storage, memory, heat, SSD health and what starts with your Mac. Plus security and backup: FileVault, the firewall, Gatekeeper, Time Machine, macOS updates, and how fresh macOS's malware definitions are.

  • Why is my Mac slow?Ten seconds of watching, compared with your usual week, and the cause by name, with one thing to do.
  • Check AppsEvery app's signature verified: Apple, App Store, a developer, or unsigned.
  • Warnings before it hurts"Your startup disk could be nearly full in about 40 days." A backup that's a week overdue. An update waiting too long.

06 · Tools and breaks

Keep it awake. Then remember to rest.

Keep Awake for a download, a build or a presentation, even with the lid closed. Breaks for your eyes in a rhythm you choose, small nudges to sit up and drink water, all quiet during calls, outside your work hours and in a Focus.

  • Keep Awake, your wayFor an hour, until the work is done, while an app is open, or with the lid closed while plugged in. It survives Warashi's own updates.
  • And the little thingsInternet and disk speed, Wi‑Fi quality, a black screen or switched-off keyboard for cleaning, hide desktop icons for a screen share, mute the microphone everywhere, Eject All, a Mac report for support.

Questions

Dobre pytania.

Kiedy mogę dostać Warashi?

Już teraz. Betę można pobrać bezpłatnie, a uaktualnia się sama. Gdy pojawi się Warashi 1.0, beta się kończy i prosi o uaktualnienie oraz zakup licencji.

Na jakich Makach działa?

Maki z Apple silicon (M1 i nowsze) z macOS 26 lub nowszym.

Czy jest bezpłatne?

Bezpłatne w czasie bety. Warashi 1.0 będzie jednorazowym zakupem, bez subskrypcji.

Czym różni się od Monitora aktywności?

Monitor aktywności pokazuje tę sekundę. Warashi pamięta: co się działo, gdy nie patrzyłeś, która aplikacja za tym stała, ile to kosztowało i co to oznacza dla Twojego następnego Maca.

Can I keep AlDente or CleanMyMac?

You can, though two charge managers will fight over the limit; keep one. Warashi tells you when it sees another one running.

Does it slow my Mac down?

No. About 0.2% of one core in the background and 60 MB of memory, measured and shown in Settings. It stops drawing altogether while your screens sleep.
