ChangelogWhat's new

Every version, in plain words.

Pre-release versions, while Warashi gets ready for launch.

0.3.53
14 Nov 2026
  • Lighter when you look: the menu bar window and the main window now redraw only the numbers that moved, so an open window costs a fraction of what it did, with the same counting numbers, sliding charts and easing rings.
  • Quieter when you don’t: while your screens sleep or the Mac is locked, Warashi stops drawing and takes a reading every 30 seconds instead of every 5.
  • Flat buttons and icons: the one primary button on each screen is solid clay, and tool icons are flat squares in their colour. Settings panes open straight on their settings.
  • Overview tiles carry their metric’s icon before the caption, in the same place on every tile; Disk and Battery centre their reading.
  • Sit up straight is a round badge with a larger figure, the words around its rim, and a slower rise from the bottom of the screen.
  • The Disk tile in the menu bar window is the Storage ring in miniature, with the same bands and colours.
  • Apps lists Warashi itself as “this window” and keeps it out of the busiest-apps rankings, so its own window never looks like a culprit.
  • Storage scans use at most half the cores, count as fresh for a day, and don’t start by themselves on battery.
  • Tools keep one fixed order; the favourites star is gone.
0.3.52
14 Nov 2026
  • Storage does more: step into any folder to see what fills it, see your Downloads oldest first with old installers marked, and find local AI models from Ollama, LM Studio and Hugging Face.
  • Remove apps properly: Storage › Apps lists every app, those you haven't opened in months first, and moves an app and what it left in your Library to the Trash, item by item, after you've seen each one. It also finds what apps you've already deleted left behind.
  • Anything you remove goes to the Trash first, so you can put it back until you empty it. Big files, downloads and your own folders can go there too.
  • Checkup: Malware protection shows how up to date XProtect is, and Check Apps checks every app and login item's signature. Local snapshots shows Time Machine's copies on the disk and can free their space. App permissions lists every privacy page in System Settings.
  • Keep Awake keeps working with the lid closed, and carries on when Warashi updates or restarts.
  • Battery: how it has aged over the months, and the Flight Log now notes when it charged past your limit or ran on battery while plugged in, naming the app that did it. A warning when another app such as AlDente also manages charging.
  • Breaks only in your work hours, paused during a Focus such as Do Not Disturb, and a switch for waiting during calls and meetings. Three new nudges: stretch, drink some water and relax your jaw.
  • Live countdowns in the notch: a running speed test, a rest coming up or a timed Keep Awake.
  • Tools: hide desktop icons for a screen share, mute the microphone in every app, and a key tester in Clean Keyboard.
  • Siri: say “Keep my Mac awake with Warashi”, “Hide desktop icons with Warashi” and more, in every language Warashi speaks.
  • Developer: running Docker containers with their CPU, memory and ports.
  • The helper checks itself and says when a restart would fix it.
0.3.51
13 Nov 2026
  • Slowdowns in the Flight Log: when your Mac struggles for 2 minutes or more, Warashi writes down how long and why, such as an app using most of the processor, memory running short, the Mac cooling off, or Spotlight indexing.
  • Three new alerts, off until you turn them on: Mac struggling, Backup overdue (Time Machine hasn't backed up for a week) and macOS update waiting (for a week).
  • Slow? in the menu bar window: one click opens Why is my Mac slow? and starts the check.
0.3.49
13 Nov 2026
  • Why is my Mac slow? In Checkup, press Feeling Slow? and Warashi watches for ten seconds, compares what it sees with your Mac's usual week, and names the likely cause, with one thing to do.
  • Security and backup in Checkup: macOS updates, Time Machine, FileVault, the firewall, System Integrity Protection and Gatekeeper, each opening its place in System Settings.
  • Breaks have their own page: click the Breaks card in Tools to see your next two hours and pick a rhythm. A new switch lets breaks come even while a video plays.
  • Fixed: a break could be cancelled at the end of its countdown when a browser was playing video.
  • The Stop Charging At card in Tools opens Battery.
  • Calmer and quicker: pages swap with one cross-fade, the menu bar window and Find anything open at once, and Checkup's verdict is a ring of what was checked.
  • Lighter in the background: less CPU and less memory.
0.3.40
12 Nov 2026
  • Break rhythms: 20-20-20, Pomodoro, 50 and 10, Deep work, or your own. Work for 15 to 120 minutes, rest for 2 to 30, with a longer rest every few rests and eye breaks in between.
  • Sit up straight now rises gently from the bottom of the screen, and Blink grows out of the notch. Choose how often each comes.
  • A redesigned heads-up before each break.
  • Empty the Trash from Health › Storage: see how much is in it, kept up to date while the page is open, and empty it after one clear question, with a count as it goes. About twice as fast on external drives.
  • Clean Screen and Clean Keyboard: a black screen to wipe, or every key switched off while you clean the keyboard. In Tools, the menu bar window and Find anything.
  • Fixed: on some Macs, Full Disk Access looked switched off even after you turned it on.
0.3.33
10 Nov 2026
  • A whole new look, in light and dark: warm, glowing glass, gradient rings and charts, and one clear button on each screen.
  • A simpler window: six places, Now, Apps, History, Health, Tools and Settings, with tabs. ⌘1–⌘5 jump between them, and each remembers where you were.
  • Find anything: press ⌘K, type a few letters, and go to any page or run an action such as Take a Break or Charge to Full.
  • Checkup: is your Mac in good shape? One answer from the last week, with battery, storage, memory, heat, SSD and external drive health, and what starts with your Mac.
  • Stop charging at the level you choose, charge to full just this once, or schedule it. In Health, Tools and, if you like, the menu bar window.
  • Tools: internet and disk speed, Wi-Fi quality, Eject All, the Trash and a Mac report to paste into a support chat.
  • Breaks: gentle reminders to rest your eyes and sit up straight, held back while you're on a call or watching something.
  • Keep Awake: for a time, until the work is done, while an app is open, or with the lid closed while plugged in. One click in the menu bar, or ⌃⌥⌘K.
  • Flight Log: what your Mac did, day by day, from last night's sleep to crashes, macOS updates and time offline.
  • New alerts: a battery running hot, a lost connection, and a public IP that changed (for example when a VPN drops).
  • The menu bar window has one-click actions and Find anything, and Settings › Customize now sets up the menu bar, the notch and the menu bar window in one place.
  • History opens far faster.
