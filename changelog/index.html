ChangelogWhat's new

Every version, in plain words.

Pre-release versions, while Warashi gets ready for launch.

0.3.3
3 Nov 2026
  • The notch and island open faster, about 0.3 seconds.
  • A slimmer notch glance, so app menus keep more room.
0.3.2
3 Nov 2026
  • The notch and island now open evenly to both sides, in one smooth motion.
0.3.1
3 Nov 2026
  • Settings moved into the window, in the same style as the rest of the app.
  • One menu bar item: the spirit and one number, with ▲ when something needs you.
  • A larger island at the top of displays without a notch, on by default.
  • A new welcome tour with animated demos.
  • Anonymous usage statistics, listed in the tour and Settings.
0.3.0
3 Nov 2026
  • A new design, Wabi: washi and lacquer colours, a new spirit logo.
  • A new window at one fixed size: Overview, Apps with a process drawer, one-chart History.
  • Notch glance and mini dashboard.
  • Hover any chart to read the value at that moment.
0.2
2 Nov 2026
  • Upgrade Advisor and Time × Cost.
  • Alerts.
  • Optional helper for system processes.
  • Welcome tour, automatic updates and licences.
0.1
1 Nov 2026
  • First version: CPU, memory, power, temperature, network, disk, battery and GPU in the menu bar and popover, per-app usage and local history.
