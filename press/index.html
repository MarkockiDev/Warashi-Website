About Warashi

Warashi is a native menu bar monitor for Apple silicon Macs, made by independent developer Adam Markocki in Poland. It records what your Mac does (CPU, memory, power and what each app costs), keeps up to a year of history on the Mac, and turns it into plain answers. It also checks the Mac's health, limits battery charging and offers small tools such as breaks and Keep Awake. Warashi is in free public beta at warashi.app.

Short description

Warashi is a calm menu bar monitor for Apple silicon Macs. It records CPU, memory, power and every app's cost, keeps a year of history on your Mac, and answers in plain words: what slowed it down, what drained the battery, and whether it's in good shape.
