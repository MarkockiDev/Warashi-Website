About Warashi

Warashi is a native menu bar app for Apple silicon Macs, made by independent developer Adam Markocki in Poland. Named after a Japanese house spirit, it looks after the whole Mac: live readings and a year of history, a battery charge limit, a weekly health Checkup, Clean Up and an uninstaller, and breaks, Soft Focus and Keep Awake for the person using it. Free public beta at warashi.app.

Short description

Warashi looks after your Mac from the menu bar: live readings and a year of history, a battery charge limit, a weekly Checkup, Clean Up and a real uninstaller, plus breaks, Soft Focus and Keep Awake. Native, private, light. Free beta.
